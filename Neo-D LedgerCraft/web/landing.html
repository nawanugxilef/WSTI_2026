The problem

Sole traders with ADHD lose receipts and miss work deductions. Not because they don't care: starting the paperwork is the hard part.

Who it affects

Australian sole traders and side-hustlers with ADHD who mix work and personal spending, often alongside a PAYG job.

Why tools don't help

Most finance apps assume you kept up. Fall behind and they show you everything at once, so the app gets closed again.

Most apps help you keep up. Neo helps you catch up.

How it works

One step for you. The AI crew does the rest.

1

Send it

A photo, a PDF or a quick note like "Bunnings hammer $45.90" in Telegram.

2

Neo sorts it

AI reads the receipt, suggests work or personal and the ATO myTax label, and checks the maths, GST and duplicates.

3

One tap to fix

Change anything with a button. Neo remembers your answer for next time. Every receipt earns XP; nothing is ever taken away.

What gets better, and how we'd measure it

Better for people

  • Work deductions captured instead of lost
  • Tax time is small steps all year, not one big dreaded task
  • No shame for falling behind

How we'd measure it

  • Receipts logged per week, per user
  • Share of receipts with a confirmed ATO label
  • Time to clear a backlog of receipts
  • Users still logging after 4 weeks

Our data

Demo receipts

Fictional receipts we made, marked [FICTIONAL DEMO]. No real people or businesses.

Tax labels

Public ATO myTax labels (for example D5 Other work-related expenses, D9 Gifts or donations, business income and expenses).

Assumptions

Amounts in AUD, GST at 10%, cash basis: income counts when received. Not tax advice; users check before they lodge.

Privacy

  • No sign-up and no personal details in the demo
  • Demo files live on the server for 30 minutes, then they're deleted
  • No sensitive or unnecessary personal information in this project
  • Each bot answers only its owner
  • Receipt originals stay in the owner's own Google Drive or on their own computer
  • Nothing connects to Council systems

Responsible AI

AI we used

  • Gemini, inside the app, reads receipt photos and notes
  • Google Antigravity, Claude and ChatGPT helped write and review the code

Where a person checks

  • The owner confirms or changes every suggestion with one tap
  • A QA agent re-checks maths, GST and duplicates; unsure receipts are marked "needs review", never posted silently
  • The owner reviews the tax summary before lodging

Known risks

  • AI can misread a shop name or amount: the details stay visible and editable
  • Wrong category: the label is shown on every receipt and is one tap to change
  • Demo photos are sent to Gemini to be read: fictional data only
